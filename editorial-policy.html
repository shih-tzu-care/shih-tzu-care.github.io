Editorial Policy

Mei Lin writes about Shih Tzus: long-coat grooming, training a small companion dog and home routines. AI tools support the drafts, and each fact links to a source Mei has read.

Mei isn't a veterinarian, and medical topics are left to vets.

Articles are revised when readers report errors via the contact page or when sources change.
